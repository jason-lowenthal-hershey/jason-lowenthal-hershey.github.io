02

The traction journey

Three stages. The right question at each one — and the platform takes founders from each answer to the next without making them start over.

Stage 01 · Ideation

Does this problem matter?

Problem / Solution Fit

Before anything is built, the question is whether anyone's pain is real and worth solving. Ideation guides founders through that work — so the next dollar of effort is spent on something the market actually wants.

Stage 02 · Incubation

Does this solution work?

Solution / Customer Fit

Once the problem is real, the test moves to whether the solution lands with the people who have it. Incubation is where the validated idea meets actual customers, and the platform helps founders run that loop fast and honestly.

Stage 03 · Acceleration

Is this a scalable business?

Product / Market Fit

When the solution is working for a real customer set, the question shifts to whether it can be a business. Acceleration is where traction compounds — and where founders move toward fundability, growth, and the conversations investors want to have.
